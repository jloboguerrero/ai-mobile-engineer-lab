# Cover letter base (EN)

Plantilla corta. El agente sustituye los `{PLACEHOLDERS}` con datos reales de la oferta.
**Nunca** enviar con placeholders sin sustituir. Si la oferta no da suficiente informacion
para llenar `{RAZON_ESPECIFICA}`, usar la version corta.

---

## Version completa (~150 palabras) — para campos de cover letter

```
Hi {EMPRESA} team,

I'm a Senior Mobile Developer with 6+ years building production Flutter apps for iOS and
Android, and I'd like to be considered for the {PUESTO} role.

Most of my work has been in fast-moving startups. At Tul, a leading Latam construction-supply
company, I built and maintained an app with over 500K downloads on a Clean Architecture +
BLoC foundation. More recently I've worked with US startups on a rideshare platform
(Riverpod, Google Maps, Firebase) and a healthcare app integrating the shenai SDK for
facial-scan vitals and Agora for live video.

{RAZON_ESPECIFICA — 1-2 frases sobre por que esta empresa/producto en concreto}

I work remotely from Colombia as an independent contractor, in UTC-5, which gives full
overlap with US business hours. Happy to walk through any of this in more detail.

Best regards,
Jonathan Lobo Guerrero
jloboguerrero@gmail.com · github.com/jloboguerrero
```

## Version corta (~60 palabras) — para campos con limite de caracteres

```
Senior Flutter developer, 6+ years, iOS and Android. Shipped an app with 500K+ downloads at
Tul (Latam) and worked with US startups on rideshare and healthcare apps — Clean Architecture,
BLoC/Riverpod, Firebase. Remote contractor from Colombia (UTC-5), full US-hours overlap,
advanced English. Would love to talk about {PUESTO}.

Jonathan Lobo Guerrero · jloboguerrero@gmail.com
```

## Reglas

- Tono directo y factual. Sin superlativos ("passionate", "world-class", "rockstar").
- Nunca afirmar experiencia que no esta en `perfil.json` (Swift nativo, React Native profesional, etc.).
- `{RAZON_ESPECIFICA}` sale de la descripcion real de la oferta — producto, dominio, escala o stack.
  Si no hay nada concreto que decir, omitir el parrafo antes que rellenar con generalidades.
